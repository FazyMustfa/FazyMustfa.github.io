Open and click tracking was switched off on purpose

A tracking pixel and a rewritten link are two of the clearest spam signals a filter has, and on cold traffic they cost more in deliverability than the data is worth. Both dashboards read 0% open and 0% click for exactly that reason.

The trade is real. With tracking off there is no open rate to point at when a campaign underperforms. Reply rate is the only signal left, and it is a much harder number to flatter.

Pipeline is counted at $1,000 an opportunity, the client's own deal size basis. The opportunity count is the measured number. The dollar figure is that count multiplied out.

The follow up beat the opener on fewer emails

Two steps: an opener split four ways, then a single follow up to everyone who did not reply. One screen holds the whole result.

Swipe to read it. Step 1 and Step 2, with all four opener variants broken out.

Step two sent 9,906 emails and produced 16 opportunities. Step one sent 11,595 and produced 14. It did that on a worse reply rate, 0.33% against 0.53%, so it pulled fewer replies and better ones. Most operators stop after the opener, and on this campaign that would have left more than half the pipeline on the table.

The variants say the same thing. Variant C pulled the most replies of any opener, 16, and produced a single opportunity. Variant D pulled 19 and produced seven, half of every step one opportunity in the campaign, off a quarter of the volume. D is the only opener in the next campaign, and the volume behind the other three goes to it.

What 3,000,000 sends a day looks like from the inside

I have been inside an account running up to 3,000,000 emails a day. It was not my account, the prospecting was not mine, and I was not there for the whole window this chart covers.

What it gave me is exposure. Watching how an operation at that tier is actually run is not something you get from the outside, and most people working in cold email have never seen it.

List supply, inbox management and deliverability stop being the same job somewhere above a few hundred thousand sends a day. I have seen where they break.

The totals on this dashboard are void. Campaigns were deleted to free contact storage and took their history with them. Only the daily volume survived, so only the daily volume is shown.
